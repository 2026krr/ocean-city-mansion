Getting Here

The Neighborhood

Ocean City Mansion sits at 416 Central Avenue, in the heart of Ocean City's historic district. Steps from the beach and boardwalk, three blocks from the Music Pier, and around the corner from Asbury Avenue's shops and cafes.

Parking: Free street parking is available on Central Avenue and surrounding streets. Our on-site parking pad is reserved for drop-off and pick-up only. Please do not leave vehicles parked on the pad.

Airport: We are about 40 minutes from Atlantic City International (ACY) and about 70 minutes from Philadelphia International (PHL).
