Pet Friendly Rooms

Bring Your Dog.

Six of our twelve rooms welcome pets: Central Room 1, Surf Room 2, Atlantic Room 3, Harbor Room 6, Inlet Room 7, and Seabright Room 12. A pet fee of $100 per pet, per stay applies to cover additional cleaning and maintenance, ensuring a fresh and comfortable environment for all guests.
