GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Full check-in details, including door codes, are emailed the morning of your arrival.

Is there parking on-site?

We do not offer dedicated on-site parking, but free street parking is generally available on Central Avenue and surrounding streets. We do have an on-site parking pad reserved for drop-off and pick-up — please do not leave vehicles parked on the pad.

Are pets allowed?

Yes — six of our twelve rooms are pet-friendly: Central Room 1, Surf Room 2, Atlantic Room 3, Harbor Room 6, Inlet Room 7, and Seabright Room 12. A pet fee of $100 per pet, per stay applies to cover additional cleaning. Pets are not permitted in our other rooms.

Is breakfast included?

We do not serve breakfast, but each room is equipped with a Nespresso machine, complimentary coffee, complimentary electrolyte drinks, and an in-room fridge. Ocean City has excellent breakfast spots within a short walk.

Do you have a pool?

No — we are a boutique historic property and do not have a pool. The beach is a short walk east on Central Avenue.

Do you provide beach chairs or umbrellas?

We do not provide beach chairs or umbrellas, but we do provide complimentary beach tags in every room. Please keep them safe and return them at check-out.

How close are you to the beach and boardwalk?

We are steps from the beach and boardwalk — a short walk east on Central Avenue. The Music Pier is three blocks away, and Asbury Avenue's shops and cafes are just around the corner.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days prior to your arrival date. Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival are non-refundable.

Do you allow smoking?

Ocean City Mansion is a strictly non-smoking property. A $500 smoke-removal charge applies to any violation.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes — smart Wi-Fi is complimentary throughout the property, along with a dedicated downstairs workspace for guests who need to catch up on work.

Is there a laundry facility?

Yes — there is a shared washer and dryer available for guest use.

How do I book?

The best way to book is directly through our website — book direct for the best rates and no booking fees. You can also call us at (267) 331-2282 or email management@vichproperties.com for help.
